    # 3 is less than the maximum possible weight, and it's balanced.
    '''

    The object q is capable of flying if it is balanced (indicating it is a palindromic list) and the sum of its elements does not surpass the maximum weight w.

    # It's balanced, but 3+2+3 is more than the maximum weight.

    # 3+2+3 is less than the maximum weight, and it's balanced.

    i, j = 0, len(q)-1
